Wedding CRM – Features Guide & Promotion Kit
Created by Panos Iliopoulos & Beyondfilms · Version 1.21.34
What it is
Wedding CRM is a complete business tool built by a working wedding photographer, for wedding photographers and videographers. It is a single file that runs on Windows 11 in Microsoft Edge (or Chrome). There is nothing to install and no account to create, and your clients' data stays on your own PC.
It covers the whole journey: the first enquiry, the offer, the contract, the deposit, the wedding day, the delivery, the review and the next referral.
The strongest features
1.	Today dashboard. One screen that tells you what needs attention: a countdown to your next event, today's events, money to collect, installments due, deliveries due, leads to follow up, travel still to book, team missing, SwissTransfer links about to expire, low disk space, subscriptions ending, to-dos, review requests, wedding anniversaries and your Calendly bookings. You can drag the cards into your own order.
2.	Everything about an event in one window. Couple, groom's and bride's phones and addresses, ceremony and reception locations, a map and the weather forecast, package, services with delivery deadlines, team, timeline, finances, travel, the drive where the media is saved, and the gallery and SwissTransfer links.
3.	Money under control. Price, deposit, payments by method, an installment schedule, expenses with quick presets, team fees added automatically, the remaining balance, profit, and an alert showing what is still to collect whenever you open an event.
4.	Professional PDFs in one click. Invoice or proforma, contracts in English and Greek with your signature, printable forms for couples, a day sheet for the wedding day, a schedule for each team member, and PDF lists of events, leads, customers, subscriptions and contacts.
5.	Ready messages. Templates for offers, follow-ups, payment reminders, thank-you and anniversary messages, filled in automatically with the couple's details and sent through WhatsApp, Viber or email. A button also prepares a ready prompt for Claude or ChatGPT.
6.	Delivery and storage tracking. Tick each service as delivered, see what is left to deliver, and keep track of your hard drives with the free space colour-coded red, yellow or green, plus which drive holds each event.
7.	Business growth tools. A colour-coded lead pipeline with follow-up dates, lead sources and conversion, price packages, social media follower growth, reviews to ask for, and anniversary reminders for repeat business.
8.	Privacy and ownership. Your data lives on your PC. Logins are kept in an encrypted vault protected by a master password. Backups can be saved automatically into your Google Drive folder. You can change themes and fonts to match your style.
Full feature guide
Today
•	Countdown to your next event, with the place and time.
•	Cards for today, the next 7 days, money to collect, installments due, follow-ups, anniversaries, review requests, deliveries, travel, team, to-dos, subscriptions, social media updates, SwissTransfer links, low storage and Calendly bookings.
•	Drag the cards to reorder them. Each row opens the record, and some rows have a one-click message button.
Reports
•	Choose a year and see total projects, total revenue, average project value, pending revenue, total clients, top client, received, expenses and profit.
•	Pie charts for sales by category, expenses against profit, payments by method, and collected against still to collect. Bars by month. Hover over any chart for the exact numbers.
•	Team workload, social media growth, lead sources with conversion, and the next events.
•	Export the report to Excel.
Leads
•	A pipeline with your own statuses and colours, shown as tinted rows with badges.
•	A dated update history for each lead, added automatically when the status changes or by hand.
•	Next follow-up date, interest in a package, source, Viber and WhatsApp.
•	Messages from templates, conversion into a client and an event in one click, a warning if the date is already booked, and a PDF export.
Customers
•	Contact details, full address, tax number and tax office, and whether they use Viber or WhatsApp.
•	Number of events and total sold for each customer. PDF export.
Events
•	Choose which columns the list shows, filter by year with the count shown, see the weekday next to each date, and export the list as PDF.
•	Inside each event: category, couple, wedding planner, groom's and bride's mobile and address, dates and times, ceremony and reception, Out of Base travel (hotel, air, ferry, car, with the company), people needed and the team assigned, timeline, services with delivery deadlines, package, deposit and payments, installment schedule, expenses, media drives, gallery link, SwissTransfer link and upload date, and a Review asked tick.
•	Buttons for Invoice (PDF), Contract, Day sheet (PDF), Google Calendar and a map.
Calendar
•	A monthly view with events, Greek public holidays and Orthodox Easter, and your own to-dos.
•	Add to Google Calendar from an event, or export all events as an .ics file.
Forms
•	Build printable forms with your logo and a title, for example a couple's details questionnaire.
•	Fill in what you already know and leave the rest blank for the client to write by hand.
Media Storage
•	List your hard drives with the remaining space, shown in red, yellow or green, and the total free space across all drives.
Subscriptions
•	Platforms and software you pay for, such as vendor listings, with start and end dates, the fee, and the cost per month and per year.
Marketing
•	Social accounts with dated follower updates, and a growth chart in Reports.
Analytics
•	Show your Google Analytics report inside the CRM through a Looker Studio link.
B2B Contacts
•	Partners and businesses such as printers and venues, with categories you define, phone, email, website and address. PDF export.
Logins
•	Platform, username and password saved in an encrypted vault. It locks automatically.
Settings
•	Your business details, logo and signature, invoice numbering, VAT and bank details.
•	22 colour themes and 8 font styles.
•	Your own event categories, services, payment methods, team with contacts and fees, expense presets, B2B categories, price packages and lead statuses.
•	Delivery deadlines per service, timeline templates, contract templates in English and Greek, message templates, the events list columns, and a Calendly connection.
•	Backups: save a backup, auto-backup into a folder such as Google Drive, and import.
Getting started in 10 minutes
1.	Save WeddingCRM.html in a folder you will keep.
2.	Make a shortcut that opens it as its own window in Edge.
3.	In Settings, add your business details, logo and signature.
4.	Set up your categories, services, payment methods and team.
5.	Choose a backup folder, for example in Google Drive.
6.	Add your first lead and convert it into a client and an event.
7.	Open the event, add the package, deposit and timeline, and create the contract.

Questions people will ask
•	Do I need to install anything? No. You open one file in Edge or Chrome.
•	Does it need internet? Most of it works offline. Internet is needed for the weather, maps, Calendly, Analytics and the AI buttons.
•	Where is my data? On your own PC. You can save backups, and set up automatic backups into your Google Drive folder.
•	Can I use it on my phone or on two computers? Not yet. For now it is for one PC. You can move your data with a backup and import.
•	Is it safe for client details? The data stays on your PC. Logins are encrypted with a master password. Anyone who uses your PC could open the CRM, so protect your PC with a password.
•	Are the invoices legally valid? In Greece, tax invoices must be issued through certified invoicing software or a myDATA provider. The PDF works as a proforma or payment request unless your accountant confirms otherwise. The contract wording is a draft that a lawyer should review.
•	Does it have AI? It prepares a ready prompt and opens Claude or ChatGPT for you. You don't need an API key.
•	Can I change it to fit my work? Yes: categories, services, payment methods, statuses, templates, themes, fonts and the columns of every list.
•	What does it cost? [add your price, or write that it is free and people can support you with a coffee].
Be honest when you promote it
People trust a clear and honest description more than a long promise. Mention these points before they ask:
•	It is for Windows, in Edge or Chrome, and for one PC at the moment, with no phone app and no automatic sync between devices.
•	The Calendly connection and the weather and map features need internet, and the Calendly connection is still new.
•	The invoice PDF is not a certified tax document, and the contract wording needs a lawyer's review before use.
